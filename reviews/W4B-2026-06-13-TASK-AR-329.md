## Important issue caught by W4b (now FIXED) — RESOLVED

backlog_board.py` mirrored the registry WRITER (sync_taskset_registry) but NOT the
registry READER merge. In downstream host projects a UI-created taskset would
render as "Unclassified" instead of its registered name — violating the
registry↔board consistency acceptance criterion for template consumers (root repo
was fine). FIX (integrator, inst-w4b-ar329-verifier, distinct from worker):
ported `_load_task_set_registry` + `_task_set_info_map` into the template and
threaded `root` through `task_set_info`/`task_set_sort_key`/`task_sets_for`/
`render`; added regression guard `test_template_backlog_board_merges_registry_reader`
asserting the template board resolves a registered taskset name + renders it.
