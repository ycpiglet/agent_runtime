## Accepted Contract

- Accepted values: numeric `TASK-N`, numeric `TASK-AR-N`, and
  `TASK-AR-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS-XXXXXXXX` with an eight-character hexadecimal
  suffix.
- Timestamp suffix letters may be lowercase or uppercase and are returned
  unchanged by the builder and token extractor.
- `TASK-<timestamp>-<hex>` without `-AR` is rejected.
- Matches embedded in larger alphanumeric, hyphenated, or underscore-bearing
  tokens are rejected.
- `task_identity`, `taskset_dispatcher`, and `conversation_work_audit` use the
  shared contract in both the live root and generated-host template surfaces.
