# Business Operating System Compound Note

## Reusable Lesson

Business-function requests become unsafe when they skip role boundaries:
finance can drift into payment/accounting mutations, marketing can drift into
platform manipulation, sales can drift into unsolicited outreach, and support
can drift into direct customer communication. The durable prevention is not a
chat warning; it is an org model plus a packet that gates external effects and
requires evidence artifacts.

## Feed-Forward

- Future business cycles should start by reading
  `agents/project/BUSINESS-OPERATING-SYSTEM.md`.
- If the work needs implementation, first register a new taskset and unit; do
  not expand an existing worker claim into adjacent business areas.
- If the work touches customers, leads, accounting systems, CRM, support desk,
  payments, contracts, or external publishing, stop at draft/evidence and ask
  for Owner approval through the recorded workflow.
