## Runnable Unit Contract

The selector classifies every sibling unit before dispatch:

1. `in_progress` and legacy `active` have live-execution priority. More than
   one such unit is an ambiguity and stops planning.
2. `worker_ready` and legacy `ready` are the next priority.
3. `planned` and legacy `assigned` are the final runnable priority.
4. When there is no live in-progress unit, a repo-relative canonical
   `task.unit_spec` wins if and only if it resolves inside that task's unit
   registry and names a runnable sibling.
5. `blocked`, `hold`, `cancelled`/`canceled`, `rejected`, `failed`,
   `planner_refine_required`, `proposed`, review states, and completed/released
   states are never dispatch candidates.
6. Missing or unknown status, a malformed canonical path, multiple
   in-progress units, or a registry with no runnable unit exits nonzero before
   a claim command can be emitted.
7. Without a canonical preference, the remaining choice is deterministic by
   runnable priority and repository-relative unit path.

The unit dependency gate, requested model tier, escalation signals,
stop condition, and generated claim arguments are derived only after this
selection and only from the selected unit.
