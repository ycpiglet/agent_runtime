# Work Hierarchy Numbering And Recording Meeting

## Bottom Line

- Summary: recorded the Owner/Claude/Codex discussion about hierarchy naming,
  generated numbering, Routine/Spike work types, and mandatory planning records.
- Result: human-facing numbers are generated by a classifier; planners should
  not reserve `task 1/2/3` style labels by hand.

## Insight

- Manual numbering is the wrong concurrency boundary. If multiple panes choose
  `TASK-AR-375` or "task 3" directly, conflict already happened before gates
  can help.
- Stable file identity should stay UUID/timestamp backed. Human-recognition
  numbers should be generated after registration from the current hierarchy.
- Milestone, horizon, team, owner, role, priority, and phase are reporting axes,
  not extra hierarchy levels.
- Routine work has recurring semantics and should not be forced into a
  close-once task tree. Spike work is time-boxed decision work and should have a
  stop boundary.

## Decision

- Decision: keep the four-level tree: Initiative, Taskset, Task, Unit.
- Decision: use generated labels: `Initiative 1`, `Taskset 1.1`,
  `Task 1.1.1`, `Unit 1.1.1.1`.
- Decision: keep `project_id` for host/repository/product identity, not as the
  taskset parent name.
- Decision: add a planning-discussion trigger so hierarchy, numbering, workflow,
  or PM-design talk must be recorded in `reviews/`.
- Decision: treat Routine and Spike as first-class work types outside or beside
  the tree, not as new hierarchy levels.
