## Immutable execution receipt

`agent-runtime-execution-receipt/v1` is emitted by:

- `auto_dispatch.py` for completion, provider error, claim loss, and budget
  skips;
- `agent_worker.py` for routed and unrouted completion/error paths, including
  a budget-blocked claimed inbox item;
- `codex_subagent_bridge.py` for single replies, council members, and
  pre-spawn budget rejection.

Each receipt has a dispatch-derived receipt ID and carries task/claim/role,
requested and selected tier, resolved model/reasoning and their sources,
observed provider/model/reasoning, input/output tokens, billed cost/currency,
route/application status, baseline evidence, and the persistent budget
preflight result. JSONL append is lock-protected, flushed, and `fsync`ed.
Duplicate dispatch IDs are refused, so a retry cannot rewrite or append a
second execution truth.

The legacy evaluation row remains readable for compatibility, but every new
execution path uses the canonical receipt. A budget-blocked worker closes its
already-claimed inbox message with a skipped receipt and reply, preventing an
orphaned claim without calling the provider.

## Persistent budget and savings integrity

- Explicit task and claim token ceilings are resolved at claim creation and
  projected with the claim.
- Preflight recomputes cumulative usage from the durable ledger, so a new
  process observes prior receipts.
- Zero, malformed, or negative configured budgets fail closed. A configured
  budget without a positive dispatch ceiling also fails closed.
- An unaffordable dispatch is rejected before provider invocation, and the
  rejection itself is recorded once.
- Savings eligibility requires an observed comparable baseline, observed
  actual usage, and a real route-identity change. Equivalent routes, configured
  baselines, unknown actual usage, or mismatched cost currencies contribute no
  savings.

No live provider was called for this W4a. Consequently this review makes no
claim about realized token or monetary savings.
